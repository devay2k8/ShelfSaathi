ShelfSaathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

ShelfSaathi helps you organise household items, locations, stock, purchases, warranties and reminders. This policy describes the Android app and its optional online features.

Your inventory and files

Inventory, Home and location names, purchase details, history, preferences, photos and attached documents are stored on your device. ShelfSaathi has no account service or developer-operated inventory server. Location names describe places you enter; the app does not request GPS location permission.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan barcodes and labels or take photos when you choose these features.
  • Notifications: show reminders when permitted by Android and your app settings.
  • Internet: optional product-image lookup and Google ML Kit services described below.
  • Photo and document selection: Android selection and sharing tools grant access to files you choose.

Scanning and Smart Capture

Google ML Kit processes input images, text and barcode data on the device. Google states that ML Kit does not send those inputs or processing outputs to its servers. ML Kit can contact Google for models, updates and compatibility information and send performance and usage metrics. Smart Capture may download a model through Google Play services.

See Google ML Kit privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

Optional online product images

When you select Find online, the app sends the product barcode to Open Food Facts or its related product databases and downloads an available image. These services receive network information such as your IP address and the app's request headers. This lookup does not upload your inventory, personal photos or documents.

Backups, exports and sharing

You can export inventory or create backups, including photos and documents where selected. Files go to the destination you choose; a cloud storage provider or recipient you select handles those copies under its own terms. Android device backup is enabled and may also back up app data according to your device settings. Backups and exports may contain sensitive personal information; store and share them carefully.

Feedback

Send feedback opens your email application addressed to puneety2k25@gmail.com. The draft includes the app version, device model and Android version. You can edit it before sending. Nothing is emailed automatically. If you send it, your email provider and the recipient receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose. Feedback is used to respond and investigate app issues.

Advertising

This version is ad-free and contains no advertising SDK. ShelfSaathi does not sell your inventory data or use it for targeted advertising.

Control and retention

You can edit or delete records through the app. Finished history, retained documents and restore safety copies may remain according to the app's features and your choices. Disabling a feature does not delete its saved data. Android's Clear storage or uninstall removes app-local data; exported files, device/cloud backups and previously sent emails are separate copies and must be managed separately. Contact us to request deletion of feedback you have sent.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact puneety2k25@gmail.com. We will update this page when relevant app practices change.
